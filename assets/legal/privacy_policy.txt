PRIVACY POLICY FOR EDUCATIONAL LEARNING APP

Last Updated: December 2024

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to our Educational Learning App ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and protect your information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our educational application.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Personal Information
- Account information (username, email address if provided)
- Educational progress and performance data
- Game session statistics and achievements
- User preferences and settings

2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- Device information (device type, operating system, app version)
- Usage analytics (features used, time spent, interaction patterns)
- Technical data (crash reports, error logs, performance metrics)
- Network information (IP address, connection type)

2.3 Information from Third Parties
We do not collect personal information from third parties without your explicit consent.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Educational Services
- Provide personalized learning experiences
- Track and display educational progress
- Generate performance reports and recommendations
- Adapt difficulty levels based on performance

3.2 App Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Develop new educational content
- Enhance user experience

3.3 Communication
- Send important app updates and notifications
- Provide customer support
- Respond to user inquiries and feedback

4. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

4.1 We Do Not Sell Personal Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.

4.2 Service Providers
We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in:
- App analytics and performance monitoring
- Cloud storage and data backup
- Customer support services
- Technical infrastructure maintenance

4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law or to:
- Comply with legal processes or government requests
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Prevent fraud or security threats
- Enforce our Terms of Service

4.4 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

5.1 Age Restrictions
Our app is designed for educational use by children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5.2 Parental Consent
For users under 13 years of age:
- We obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information
- Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information
- Parents can refuse further collection or use of their child's information

5.3 Limited Data Collection
For children under 13, we only collect information necessary for the educational functionality of the app.

6. DATA SECURITY

6.1 Security Measures
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Regular security assessments and updates
- Access controls and authentication systems
- Secure data storage and backup procedures

6.2 Data Breach Response
In the event of a data breach, we will:
- Notify affected users within 72 hours
- Report to relevant authorities as required by law
- Take immediate steps to secure the breach
- Provide guidance on protective measures

7.1 Access and Control
You have the right to:
- Access your personal information
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your personal information
- Export your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent for data processing

7.2 Communication Preferences
You can:

- Adjust notification settings
- Control data sharing preferences

7.3 Account Deletion
You can delete your account at any time, which will:

8.1 Retention Period
