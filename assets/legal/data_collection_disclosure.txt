DATA COLLECTION DISCLOSURE FOR EDUCATIONAL LEARNING APP

Last Updated: December 2024

This disclosure provides detailed information about the data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

1. OVERVIEW OF DATA COLLECTION

1.1 Purpose
We collect data to:
- Provide personalized educational experiences
- Track learning progress and performance
- Improve our educational content and features
- Ensure app security and functionality
- Comply with legal requirements

1.2 Data Collection Principles
- Minimal Data Collection: We only collect data necessary for app functionality
- Transparency: We clearly explain what data we collect and why
- User Control: You have control over your data and privacy settings
- Security: We implement strong security measures to protect your data
- Legal Compliance: We comply with all applicable privacy laws

2. TYPES OF DATA COLLECTED

2.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Username or display name
- Email address (if provided)
- Age range or date of birth (for age-appropriate content)
- Parental consent status (for users under 13)
- Account creation date
- Profile preferences and settings

2.2 Educational Progress Data
To track your learning progress, we collect:
- Quiz and test scores
- Time spent on activities
- Learning paths and subjects accessed
- Difficulty levels attempted
- Achievement and milestone data
- Study session duration and frequency
- Question response patterns
- Performance analytics and trends

2.3 Usage Analytics
To improve our app, we collect:
- App usage patterns and frequency
- Feature usage statistics
- Navigation paths within the app
- Time spent in different sections
- Error logs and crash reports
- Device performance metrics
- User interaction patterns

2.4 Device Information
We may collect:
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Screen resolution and size
- Device language settings
- Unique device identifiers (anonymized)
- Network connection type

2.5 Location Data
- We do NOT collect precise location data
- We may collect general region information for content localization
- Location data collection requires explicit user consent

2.6 Communication Data
If you contact us, we may collect:
- Support request details
- Feedback and suggestions
- Communication preferences
- Response history

3. DATA COLLECTION METHODS

3.1 Direct Collection
- Information you provide when creating an account
- Data entered during app usage
- Feedback and support communications
- Survey responses (if applicable)

3.2 Automatic Collection
- Usage analytics through app interactions
- Performance data during app use
- Error logs and diagnostic information
- Device information upon app installation

3.3 Third-Party Sources
- App store information (for downloads and updates)
- Authentication services (if using social login)
- Payment processors (for premium features)

4. SPECIAL PROTECTIONS FOR CHILDREN

4.1 COPPA Compliance
For users under 13 years of age:
- Parental consent is required before data collection
- We collect minimal information necessary for app functionality
- Parents can review, modify, or delete their child's information
- We do not share children's data with third parties for marketing

4.2 Enhanced Privacy Measures
- Stricter data minimization practices
- Additional security safeguards
- Regular review of data collection practices
- Clear parental controls and oversight options

5. DATA USAGE PURPOSES

5.1 Primary Uses
- Providing educational content and features
- Personalizing learning experiences
- Tracking progress and performance
- Generating achievement reports
- Maintaining account security

5.2 Secondary Uses
- Improving app functionality and user experience
- Developing new educational content
- Analyzing usage patterns for optimization
- Ensuring technical performance and reliability
- Complying with legal obligations

5.3 Marketing and Communications
- We do NOT use personal data for targeted advertising
- We may send educational content recommendations
- All communications can be opted out of
- We respect user communication preferences

6. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

6.1 No Sale of Personal Data
We do NOT sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

6.2 Limited Sharing Scenarios
We may share data only in these circumstances:
- With parental consent (for children under 13)
- To comply with legal requirements
- To protect user safety and security
- With service providers under strict confidentiality agreements
- In case of business transfer (with user notification)

6.3 Service Providers
We may share data with trusted service providers for:
- Cloud storage and data processing
- Analytics and performance monitoring
- Customer support services
- Payment processing (for premium features)

All service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

7. DATA RETENTION

7.1 Retention Periods
- Account data: Retained while account is active
- Progress data: Retained for educational continuity
- Usage analytics: Aggregated and anonymized after 2 years
- Support communications: Retained for 3 years
- Deleted account data: Permanently deleted within 30 days

7.2 Data Deletion
You can request deletion of your data at any time through:
- App settings
- Email request to privacy@educationalapp.com
- Parental request (for children's accounts)

8. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

8.1 Technical Safeguards
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Secure authentication systems
- Regular security audits and updates
- Access controls and monitoring
- Secure data centers and infrastructure

8.2 Organizational Safeguards
- Employee training on data protection
- Strict access controls and need-to-know basis
- Regular privacy and security assessments
- Incident response procedures
- Vendor security requirements

9. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

9.1 Access Rights
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Review data collection practices
- Understand how your data is used
- Request copies of your data

9.2 Control Rights
You can:
- Update or correct your information
- Delete your account and data
- Opt out of certain data collection
- Adjust privacy settings
- Withdraw consent (where applicable)

9.3 Parental Rights (for children under 13)
Parents can:
- Review their child's information
- Request deletion of child's data
- Refuse further data collection
- Contact us with privacy concerns
