## 📸 **How to Capture Screenshots from Android Emulator**

### **Method 1: Emulator Built-in Screenshot**
1. **Locate the camera icon** in the emulator toolbar (right side)
2. **Click the camera icon** to take a screenshot
3. **Screenshots are saved** to your default Pictures folder
4. **Rename files** according to the naming convention above

### **Method 2: Keyboard Shortcut**
1. **Press `Ctrl + S`** while the emulator is focused
2. **Choose save location** and filename
3. **Ensure PNG format** for best quality

### **Method 3: Extended Controls**
1. **Click the "..." (more)** button in emulator toolbar
2. **Select "Screenshot"** from the menu
3. **Save with appropriate filename**
